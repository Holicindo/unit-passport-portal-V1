## 💡 Strategic Approach

### Core Themes (Unique Positioning)

**Kami tidak hanya bicara produk — kami bicara pengalaman:**

```
🔦 PENCAHAYAAN               🌡️ SUHU RUANGAN              ⭐ PELAYANAN DETAIL
├─ Cahaya & mood             ├─ Suhu & kepuasan           ├─ Detail kecil yang diperhatikan
├─ Warna cahaya & persepsi   ├─ Standar suhu ideal        ├─ Respons cepat
├─ Kesalahan umum            ├─ Studi kasus bisnis        ├─ Customer-centric service
└─ Tips praktis              └─ Cara mengukur             └─ Formula kepuasan (pillar)
```

**Why This Works:**
- ✅ Kompetitor fokus pada produk/harga — **kita fokus pada customer experience**
- ✅ Konten edukatif → membangun **thought leadership**
- ✅ Multi-platform → menjangkau customer di berbagai touchpoint
- ✅ Google Review strategy → **konversi kepuasan jadi social proof**

## ⚠️ Risk Mitigation

### Potential Risks & Mitigation Strategy

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| **Konten tidak terindex Google** | Medium | High | Submit manual ke GSC, build backlink |
| **LinkedIn engagement rendah** | Medium | Medium | A/B test hook, optimize posting time |
| **Customer enggan kasih review** | High | High | Train staf, timing strategy, QR code |
| **YouTube views rendah** | Medium | Low | Optimize thumbnail, cross-promote di blog/LinkedIn |
| **Kompetitor copy strategy** | Low | Medium | Build moat: consistent publishing + authenticity |

**Overall Risk Level:** 🟡 **MEDIUM** — Manageable dengan monitoring & adjustment
